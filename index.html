Where this comes from. Pulled directly from the ESPN Fantasy API for league 169951, all sixteen seasons (2011–2026), then cross-checked. Champions were confirmed two independent ways — ESPN's final calculated rank and the winner of the last game in the winners' bracket — and every season agrees.

Two honest caveats. 2011 ran two-NFL-week playoff matchups, so twelve of its games are two weeks of scoring combined; those are excluded from single-week records but still count as wins and season points. Seasons before 2018 come from ESPN's older history endpoint, which reports scores as whole numbers — so pre-2018 figures are accurate but less precise than the decimals from 2018 on.

2026 is in progress: the draft is done and standings are live, but it has no champion yet, so it is excluded from all-time records and the finish grid.
